Kinride Privacy Policy

Beta version · Effective 28 October 2026

Beta policy. This policy covers the closed, invite-only Kinride beta. It will be reviewed by a lawyer and updated before Kinride is released publicly.

Kinride is operated by Muroc LLC ("we", "us"). Kinride is a carpool app for verified university students, and to work it has to know who you are and, during trips, where the car is. This page explains what we collect, why, where it is kept, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it.

The short version: we collect only what the carpool needs, we use it only to run Kinride and keep members safe, and we never sell it, never show ads and never track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

The camera and location are only used after you allow them in iOS. Location permission is "While Using", never "Always".

Who can see what

  • Other signed-in members of your university community (except people you have blocked or who blocked you) can see your first name and last initial, profile photo, bio, average rating (once you have 3 or more), number of rides, when you joined, whether you joined by referral, and your verification badges. For drivers they also see the car's make, model and colour and the trips you post.
  • Riders with a confirmed seat on a driver's trip also see the plate number and the car photo and, during the live trip, the driver's location.
  • Your driver sees your exact pickup and drop-off addresses only after your booking is confirmed. Other riders never see them.
  • The people in a booking (the rider and the driver) see their messages. Ratings stay hidden until both sides have rated or 72 hours have passed.
  • Anyone you send a "Share trip" link to can see the driver's first name, the car (including the plate), the route, the times and the car's live position. The link stops working no later than 1 hour after the trip ends, and right away if the trip is cancelled or the person who shared it no longer has a seat.
  • Kinride admins (the people at Muroc LLC who run the service) can see account details including your email and date of birth, driver documents, who invited whom, reports, emergency alerts and an audit log of admin actions. The admin tools do not show your chats or your addresses. People who maintain the service can technically access the database, and do so only to run, fix or secure Kinride or to investigate a safety report.
  • We never show other members your email, full last name, date of birth, documents or who invited you.

Service providers

We use a few companies to run Kinride. They process data on our behalf, only to provide their service:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in, file storage and live updates, in the United States (AWS us-east-1). Data is encrypted at rest. Photos and documents are in private storage, and the app opens them only through links that expire after 5 minutes.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers the one-time sign-in codes, so your email address and the code pass through Google's mail servers.
  • Apple distributes the beta through TestFlight, provides maps, address search and driving distances through Apple Maps (addresses you type are sent to Apple to find them on the map), and will deliver push notifications. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Stripe (Stripe, Inc., United States) is our payment processor. Riders pay Muroc LLC through Stripe; we hold the payment and, 48 hours after the ride, send the driver's share to the driver's Stripe account, and Stripe pays it to the driver's bank. Stripe receives the card details you type into its form, the amounts, an internal user ID, and, for drivers, the details entered on Stripe's pages and the email address we pass when the driver starts setting up payouts. Stripe's form in the app also collects device and usage information about the form for fraud prevention and to run and improve it. Stripe uses this data as described in Stripe's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website, the shared-trip page and the page that returns drivers to the app after Stripe's payout setup. GitHub may log the IP address of visitors.

We do not sell or rent your personal data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for advertising. Kinride has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools of its own; the only outside code in the app that collects anything is Stripe's payment form described above.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or if we believe in good faith that it is needed to protect someone's safety, for example in response to a valid request from law enforcement after a reported incident.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile › Delete account, then Continue and confirm. This permanently deletes your profile and photo, driver documents and car details, addresses, messages, ratings and invite codes, cancels your upcoming trips and bookings (the other people are notified; a paid seat follows the same refund rules as cancelling it in the app, and refunds and payouts already due still go through) and signs you out. We keep only the records listed in the table above: safety reports, emergency alerts and the admin log entry for the deletion, the inviter's code marked as used (with your user ID only), payment records, and, if you drove, the price calculations for your trips (with your user ID only). If you set up payouts, your Stripe account stays with Stripe; you can close it from Stripe's dashboard. After deletion you can join again only with a new invite. If you can't use the app, email us from your university address and we will delete your account.

Your choices and rights

  • You can see and edit your profile in the app, and turn off camera, location or notifications in iOS Settings at any time (some features then won't work, such as starting a trip as a driver).
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing us. We will answer within 30 days.

Security

Every table in our database has access rules that limit each member to the data described above, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and documents are kept in private storage. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will tell you.

Age

Kinride is only for people aged 18 or older. Sign-up is blocked for anyone whose date of birth shows they are under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we will delete it.
