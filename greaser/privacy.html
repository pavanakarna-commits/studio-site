Greaser Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

Greaser does not collect, store on servers, or share personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Flight recordings (location, altitude and motion-sensor data), aircraft profiles, scores, notes and logbook entries are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location and motion

Greaser uses your location, the barometer and motion sensors only while you have armed or started a recording, so it can reconstruct your flight. This data is processed on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else.

Weather lookup

To show the wind at the time of your landing, Greaser can request a historical weather report (METAR) from the U.S. National Weather Service's public service at aviationweather.gov. The request contains only the airport weather-station identifier and a time window — no personal information, no device identifier and no flight track. You can turn this off in Settings and enter wind manually.

Maps

Flight maps are drawn with Apple Maps (MapKit). To show the map, your device downloads map tiles from Apple for the area being displayed, as any app using Apple Maps does. Apple handles those requests under Apple's privacy policy; we receive nothing. Without a connection, the map background is simply blank.

Sharing and export

Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share it — for example a share card image, a logbook CSV, or a raw flight data file — using the iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes.

Keep in mind that a flight track or share card can reveal where you fly from, such as your home airport. Share cards let you hide the tail number, airport and track before sharing.

Requests for data

Because we hold no flight data or personal data about you, we have nothing we could provide to anyone, including in response to legal or government requests. Your recordings exist only on your device and in any files you choose to share.

Children

Greaser does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
