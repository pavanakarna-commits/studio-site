Greaser

Pilot Landing Score — automatic, honest flight debriefs on your iPhone.

Greaser records your flight from your iPhone (in your pocket or on a yoke/glare-shield mount), splits it into phases and, after shutdown, gives you a debrief: a Landing Score for every landing, a stabilized-approach check, and a few things to work on. Every number shows how confident the app is in it, and anything it can't measure well is marked “not scored” instead of guessed.

Not a navigation or safety device. Greaser is a post-flight review tool for entertainment and self-improvement. Never use it for navigation, flight decisions or to replace instruction, the POH, or your own judgment.

Tips

  • Tap Arm before taxi; recording then starts and stops automatically.
  • Mount the phone rigidly and run the quick level calibration to unlock touchdown softness, bank and smoothness metrics.
  • Tag each landing's type (normal, short field, soft field, crosswind…) — scoring weights depend on it.
  • Disagree with a score? Annotate or dispute it in the debrief and it will be re-scored without that segment.
