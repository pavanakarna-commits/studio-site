CFI Debrief Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

CFI Debrief does not collect, store on servers, or share personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your instructor profile, aircraft, students (including any names, phone numbers or email addresses you enter), lessons, grades, notes and debriefs are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Contacts

If you choose to add a student from your contacts, iOS shows you its own contact picker and the app receives only the contact you pick. It is saved on your device as a student record. The app does not read or upload your address book.

On-device writing help

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, CFI Debrief can use Apple's on-device language model to polish the wording of a debrief. This runs entirely on your device; the lesson is not sent to us or to a cloud service. On other devices the app writes the debrief with its own built-in templates.

Sharing and export

Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share it, for example a debrief by Messages or Mail, a PDF, or a backup file, using the iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes. Debriefs and backups contain your notes about students, so share them with care.

Requests for data

Because we hold no data about you or your students, we have nothing we could provide to anyone, including in response to legal or government requests.

Children

CFI Debrief does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
