CFI Debrief

Tap-grade flight lessons to the ACS, and send the debrief before the prop stops.

CFI Debrief is a note-taking app for flight instructors. During a lesson you grade ACS tasks and tag common errors with large, cockpit-friendly buttons instead of typing. After the flight the app turns those taps into a written debrief for your student (text, logbook remarks line or PDF) and tracks each student's progress against the Private Pilot and Instrument Rating ACS.

Instructional notes only. CFI Debrief is not an official training record, logbook or endorsement, and its readiness figures are a training aid, not a sign-off. Always follow the current FAA ACS, the FARs and your own judgment. Keep your eyes outside: the app is designed for quick glances, never at the expense of see-and-avoid.

Tips

  • Plan the lesson before engine start: the app suggests tasks the student was asked to work on, hasn't been introduced to, or is below standard on.
  • In Flight Mode, tap a task tile, then a grade. Add error or tolerance chips if you like. Everything saves instantly; Undo is always on screen.
  • Use the lock button to block stray taps in turbulence.
  • Switch handedness and the Night (red) theme in Settings.
  • Spot a wrong task, tolerance or error phrase? Use “Report a content error” on that task. We fix content fast.
