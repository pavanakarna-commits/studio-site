Fitting Nook Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

The short version: Fitting Nook does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data. Your photos, garments, results and settings stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your reference photos, garment photos, generated looks and any text you type are processed and stored only on your device, with iOS file protection. They are excluded from iCloud and device backups, so they are not uploaded there either.
  • All try-on generation and all safety checks (nudity, age and garment checks) run on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or to any AI service.
  • Your free try-on count is kept in your iPhone's Keychain.

What we never do

No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs. We never receive your photos or results.

Apple services

  • Model download. On first launch the app downloads its AI models from Apple's servers (Background Assets). This download contains no data about you; Apple handles it like an app download.
  • Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details, only whether a subscription is active.
  • Age range. If you allow it, iOS tells the app only whether your age range is 18 or over (Apple's Declared Age Range feature). The app does not receive your birth date.
See Apple's privacy policy.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose: if you export, save or share a look, it goes where you send it (for example Photos or another app). Exported images are marked as AI-generated and carry no location data. If you email us, we receive what you send and use it only to reply.

Deleting your data

Use Me › Delete everything, or delete the app. Because we hold no copy, nothing remains with us.

Children

Fitting Nook is for adults (18+) and blocks photos that appear to show children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Questions: pavanakarna@gmail.com.
