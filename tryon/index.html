Fitting Nook

Try clothes on yourself, privately, on your iPhone.

Fitting Nook shows you how a garment could look on you. Take a photo of yourself, add a photo or screenshot of a garment, and the app creates the try-on entirely on your iPhone. Your photos never leave your phone unless you choose to share or export a look.

Results are AI-generated previews. They show colour, silhouette and overall look, not exact fit or sizing. Fitting Nook is for adults (18+) and only for photos of yourself.

Tips

  • Needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer. The first launch downloads the try-on models (about 0.9 GB) from Apple; after that the app works fully offline.
  • For the best result, stand in good, even light, facing the camera, arms slightly away from your body.
  • Garment photos work best flat or on a hanger against a plain background. Use the outline brush to fix the edges.
  • Rendering takes about a minute and keeps your phone busy; it pauses briefly if the phone gets hot.
  • Free includes 2 try-ons per rolling 7 days with a small watermark. Plus and Unlimited are optional subscriptions, managed in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. Use Restore Purchases in the app after reinstalling.
  • Me › Delete everything removes all your photos, garments and looks from the app.
